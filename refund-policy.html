Refund Policy

Our commitment to ensuring customer satisfaction with clear and fair refund practices

1. General Refund Policy

At CHAND TRICKER, we strive to ensure customer satisfaction with all our services and digital products. We understand that sometimes things may not work out as expected, and we have established this refund policy to address such situations fairly and transparently.

2. Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds may be considered under the following circumstances:

  • Technical issues that prevent access to purchased services
  • Services not delivered as described on our platform
  • Duplicate payments or billing errors
  • Account-related issues beyond user control
  • Unauthorized transactions

Processing Time

Refund requests are typically processed within 5-7 business days. Once approved, the refund will be credited to your original payment method within 7-14 business days, depending on your payment provider.

3. Non-Refundable Items

The following are generally not eligible for refunds:

  • Services that have been fully delivered and used
  • Digital products after download or access
  • Subscription fees after the service period has begun
  • Services terminated due to violation of our Terms of Service
  • Requests made after 30 days from purchase date

4. Refund Request Timeline

To be eligible for a refund, you must submit your request within 30 days of the original purchase date. Requests submitted after this period will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis but are not guaranteed to be approved.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please follow these simple steps:

1

Contact Support

Reach out to our support team through the Contact Page

2

Provide Details

Share your account email and transaction details

3

Explain Reason

Describe why you're requesting a refund

4

Submit Evidence

Include any relevant screenshots or documentation

6. Partial Refunds

In some cases, we may offer partial refunds based on:

  • The amount of service already consumed
  • Time elapsed since purchase
  • The nature of the issue encountered
  • Usage duration of premium features

7. Subscription Cancellations

For subscription-based services:

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period
  • No refunds are provided for unused portions of the current billing period
  • You will retain access until the end of your paid period

8. Dispute Resolution

If you are not satisfied with the outcome of your refund request, you may:

  • Request a review by our management team
  • Contact us through our official support channels
  • Provide additional documentation to support your case

Need Help?

For refund-related inquiries, please contact us through our Contact Page or reach out via our social media channels. Our support team is available to assist you with any questions or concerns.
